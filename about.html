The Story

BORN FROM FRUSTRATION.
BUILT WITH PRECISION.

SPEKTR Racing is a community-first riding brand with racing DNA, built in Bengaluru for how Indians actually ride. Race-grade protection — designed for Indian bodies, Indian heat and Indian roads, at a price that respects your money.

Scroll
● 01 / The Manifesto

ASK THE RIGHT
QUESTION.

Something is happening on Indian roads, and it isn't subtle. Riders are chasing apexes, clubs are forming in every city, track days are selling out. India isn't catching up to global moto culture — it's building its own version of it, rawer and more honest than anything the European or American markets have produced.

And yet the gear stayed the same. Riders doing 250 kmph in jackets designed for a German winter. Heavy imports built for neither 35-degree heat, nor potholed roads, nor Indian body proportions — and the best of it costs more than most riders earn in a month.

Nobody was asking the right question. Not “how do we make cheaper gear?” but “how do we build gear actually designed for this rider, on these roads, in this climate, at a price that respects their intelligence?” SPEKTR was built to answer that.

● 02 / Operating Principles
01

RACING DNA, NOT DÉCOR

Racing DNA isn't an aesthetic — it's a standard. The same rigour that goes into a race suit informs the stitch on a hoodie. If it doesn't meet the standard, it doesn't ship.

02

BUILT FOR INDIAN BODIES

European gear is cut on European templates. We start from Indian proportions — sizing, cut and armour placement designed for the rider who actually exists here.

03

NO LOGO TAX

No brand legacy padding the price. Every rupee goes back into materials, construction and R&D. Transparency isn't optional — it's the whole point.

● 03 / Receipts

HOW WE GOT HERE.

Spark

THE JUICE STOP.

Sai and Sohail, watermelon juice after a breakfast ride, complaining about how much effort it took to gear up for a short coffee run. The idea lands: a protective hoodie — light, quick to wear, with real protection.

Alignment

THE CIRCLE AGREES.

Once every rider in their circle agreed the problem was real, there was no turning back. Safety stopped being a track-day luxury and became a daily necessity — gym, office, school run, coffee.

The Pivot

BHAMAS COFFEE.

On a Chikmagalur trip, Sagar arrives with a full startup deck — vision, revenue model, the works. Sohail's reply: “What PPT? We're starting a riding gear brand.” The deck dies at the table. Not a pivot — a recognition.

The Line

HOLD THE STANDARD.

Self-funded while holding full-time jobs, the founders walked away from manufacturers and design partners who didn't meet the standard — even when it cost time and money they couldn't easily recover.

Chapter 01

THE RANGE OPENS.

Riding hoodies, the pullover, the balaclava and the base liner — modular, armour-ready, and built for how Indians actually ride. Chapter one of a longer story.

The Horizon

THE SUIT IS COMING.

The leather race suit is in deep R&D. A racing team is a direction on the horizon. The ambition is global; the intention is patient.

● 04 / The Paddock

FOUR FOUNDERS,
ONE OBSESSION.

FNDR.01
Research / Coordination

SAI VIGNESH

"The researcher."

Glued to MotoGP as a kid, Sai bought a Royal Enfield Classic 350 with his own first earnings, then impulsively a Kawasaki Z900 after a single look at his bank balance. He lives inside the product details — the one most likely to still be studying a stitch pattern at midnight because something isn't quite right. The whole thing started with him and Sohail over watermelon juice, complaining about gearing up for a short coffee run.

"Improvement isn't a phase. It's a permanent operating mode."
FNDR.02
Roadmap / Momentum

SOHAIL

"The smooth operator."

As a kid Sohail could name a motorcycle by its sound alone. He got his first bicycle in 10th grade, then talked his way into a Hero Honda Splendor — choosing the bike over a top-grade college — and rode through nearly every significant bike in the Indian market. He holds what he openly calls a delusional vision and makes his co-founders see it: the calm voice when pressure spikes, the one who says it'll get done and quietly makes it happen.

"I'd rather build the right product than a fast one."
FNDR.03
Finance / Direction

SAGAR

"The guardian of direction."

Before a superbike ever entered the frame, Sagar played right-half on the hockey field in Kadapa — speed, discipline and teamwork came from sport. He rode regular bikes as his training ground until his wife gifted him a Kawasaki Z900 on his 35th birthday. He understands the value of money from lived experience, slows things down when speed threatens quality, and protects the vision from being diluted in the rush to grow.

"Growth matters. Direction matters more."
FNDR.04
Sourcing / Supply Chain

DEEPAK

"The jugaad man."

Deepak is the one who, by the time you go looking for him, has already solved the problem. His world is manufacturing, sourcing and supply chain — who makes what, where materials come from, which factories meet the standard and which waste your time. A hardcore rider who knows the gear problem from the saddle, not a spreadsheet, he doesn't announce what he's going to do. He makes it happen, then tells you it's done.

"I don't announce it. I make it happen."
● 05 / Where We Ride Next

THE FIGHT ISN'T
OVER.

The leather race suit is in deep R&D — it won't arrive until it's the best thing an Indian rider can get at its price. Beyond it: a full ecosystem of performance gear, technology integration, and eventually a racing team under the SPEKTR name. The ambition is global. The intention is patient.
